How To Use This Voice Of Customer Template

  1. Set the reporting period and write a two or three sentence summary of how customers feel.
  2. Enter the share of positive, neutral and negative feedback.
  3. List the main feedback trends with their sentiment, frequency and business impact.
  4. Add priority action items with an owner and a target date.
  5. Generate the summary and export it as a PDF for your leadership or product team.

Run it monthly or quarterly so you can compare trends over time.

Voice Of Customer Interview Questions

  • What made you choose us in the first place?
  • What outcome are you trying to reach this year, and how are we helping?
  • Where do you lose the most time when using the product?
  • If you could change one thing about working with us, what would it be?
  • Who else on your team should we talk to?
  • How likely are you to renew, and what would make that decision easier?

Voice Of Customer Survey Questions

  • How satisfied are you with the product overall? (1 to 5)
  • How easy is it to get value from the product? (1 to 5)
  • Which feature matters most to your team?
  • What is the one thing we should fix first?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Voice of Customer template?

A VOC template is a structured format for collecting customer feedback from calls, surveys and tickets, then turning it into trends and actions that the business can act on.

Who should own the Voice of Customer program?

Customer Success often owns it because CSMs hear feedback every day. Product, Support and Marketing should all receive the summary and own action items.

How often should you run a VOC report?

Monthly works for fast-moving teams. Quarterly fits most B2B SaaS companies and lines up with business reviews.
